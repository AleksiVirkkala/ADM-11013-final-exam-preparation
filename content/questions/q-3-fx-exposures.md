## Question

> Explain the three types of foreign-exchange exposure and give an example of each.

Teacher: *"Understand all three. Be able to provide examples."*

## Quick answer

| Type | Definition | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Transaction** | Individual deal P&L from FX changes | US airline orders Airbus A330s in EUR; €/$ moves before delivery → cost balloons |
| **Translation** | Reported financials (accounting) from consolidation | Peso depreciation lowers USD value of Mexican sub's equity on parent's balance sheet |
| **Economic** | Long-run competitive position | Strong USD → US exporters lose market share globally |

> [!important] **Economic exposure requires strategic hedging** (disperse production globally) — financial hedges work only for transaction and translation.

## Key points to hit

1. Define each type with a one-sentence explanation
2. **Give an example for each** — without examples you lose half the points
3. **Distinguish hedging tactics**: transaction + translation can be hedged with forwards, swaps, options, lead/lag. Economic exposure can ONLY be hedged via strategic moves (Toyota dispersed plants worldwide)
