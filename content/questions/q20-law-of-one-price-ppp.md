## Question

> What is the law of one price? How is it related to the concept of Purchasing Power Parity?

## Quick answer

| | Law of One Price | PPP |
|---|---|---|
| Scope | Single product | "Basket" of goods |
| Implication | Exchange rate must make that one product cost the same across countries | Exchange rate must make the basket cost the same |
| Test | Specific goods (oil, gold) | Big Mac Index (Big Mac as the basket) |

**PPP is the generalization of the law of one price to a basket of goods.**

## Key points to hit

1. **Law of one price**: identical products must sell for the same price across countries (in efficient markets, no trade barriers/transport costs)
2. **PPP**: extends to a basket of goods
3. **Big Mac Index** is The Economist's PPP test
4. Why neither holds perfectly: transport costs, tariffs, government intervention, market power, investor psychology
5. Over/undervaluation is measured by deviation from PPP
