## Question

> What was the Bretton Woods system, and why did it collapse?

## Quick answer

Post-WWII international monetary order created in **1944**:

- Every currency pegged to USD; USD pegged to gold at **\$35/oz**
- Created the [[../topics/monetary-system/imf-vs-worldbank|IMF]] (to police the system) and the [[../topics/monetary-system/imf-vs-worldbank|World Bank]] (to fund reconstruction/development)
- Devaluation rules: up to 10% without IMF approval

**Why it collapsed (1971-1973):**
- US ran inflationary monetary policy late-1960s (Vietnam War + Great Society spending)
- US trade balance turned negative in 1971 for first time since 1945
- Speculation against the dollar overwhelmed the system
- **Aug 1971**: Nixon ended dollar-gold convertibility
- **1973**: System formally collapsed; floating rates emerged
- **1976** Jamaica Agreement formalized floating, abandoned gold as reserve asset

> "Achilles' heel: the system could not work if the USD was under speculative attack."
