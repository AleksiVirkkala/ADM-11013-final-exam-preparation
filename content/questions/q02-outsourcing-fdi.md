## Question (multiple choice)

> Outsourcing or Offshore production relates to:
>
> a. **FDI undertaken by a firm to serve its home market** ← ✅
> b. FDI undertaken by a firm to serve the host market
> c. Outflow of foreign investment from developed countries into developing countries
> d. Outflow of foreign investment to developed countries from developing countries

## Answer: **A**

## Why

**Outsourcing / Offshore production = producing abroad to ship back home for sale in the home market.**

The classic example: a US company sets up a maquiladora in Mexico, manufactures components or finished goods there (taking advantage of lower labor costs), and ships them back to the US for sale.

This is **FDI (Foreign Direct Investment) undertaken by a firm to serve its home market**.

## Why the other options are wrong

- **(b) FDI to serve the host market** is a different motivation: a firm invests in a country to *sell to consumers in that country* (e.g., McDonald's in Mexico serving Mexican customers). This is **market-seeking FDI**, not outsourcing/offshoring.
- **(c) and (d)** describe direction of capital flow but don't capture the home-market vs host-market purpose distinction that defines outsourcing.
