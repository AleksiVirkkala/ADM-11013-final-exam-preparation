## Question

> What are the relative advantages and disadvantages of the Civil Law system (codified) and the Common Law system?

> [!info] Teacher dislikes "one is better than the other" stances. Present both honestly.

## Quick answer

|  | Common Law | Civil Law |
|---|---|---|
| **Pro** | Flexible; judges adapt to unique cases; evolves with society | Predictable; cheaper contracts; less adversarial; faster |
| **Con** | Costly contracts (must spell out everything); adversarial; less predictable | Inflexible; harder to adapt to new situations |

## Key points to hit

1. Brief definition of each system (basis + where used)
2. Common law: judge-made, precedent-based, flexible; UK/US/Canada
3. Civil law: code-based, judges apply not interpret; Germany/France/Mexico
4. Pros/cons table (above) — discuss each side
5. **Bottom line: neither is unambiguously better.** Reasonable people disagree.
