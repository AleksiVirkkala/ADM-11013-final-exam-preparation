## Question

> What are the roles of the IMF and the World Bank?

Teacher: *"Must know."*

## Quick answer

- **IMF** maintains **monetary stability** via short-term loans + macro-policy oversight during currency/banking/foreign-debt crises
- **World Bank** finances **long-term economic development** in poorer countries

Both created at the [[../topics/monetary-system/bretton-woods|Bretton Woods]] conference in 1944; both headquartered in Washington, D.C.

## Key points to hit

1. Both founded at Bretton Woods 1944
2. **IMF**: monetary stability; short-term crisis loans (currency/banking/foreign-debt); policy conditions (anti-inflation, austerity)
3. **World Bank** (officially IBRD): long-term development; two arms (IBRD market-rate + IDA 0%-interest for poorest countries)
4. Crisis examples: Brazil 2002, Iceland 2008, Greece post-2008
5. **Moral hazard** as a critique of IMF bailouts
