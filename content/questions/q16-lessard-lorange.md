## Question

> What does the Lessard-Lorange model have to say about budgeting and comparison of actual results to the budget?

## Quick answer

Use the **Projected (forward) rate** to translate **BOTH** budget AND actual (combination **PP**). Same rate on both sides eliminates FX-distortion in performance measurement.

## Full essay answer

See [[../topics/mnc-finance/lessard-lorange#What to say on the exam|Lessard-Lorange page — full essay]].

## Key points to hit

1. **The problem**: international budgets in home currency get distorted by FX movements during the year
2. **3 rates**: Initial (I, when budget set), Projected (P, forward/forecast), Ending (E, actual at comparison time)
3. **Recommendation: combination PP** — projected rate for both budget AND actual
4. **Why PP**: same rate on both sides eliminates within-year FX distortion; uses forward-looking forecast
5. Of the 9 possible combinations, 5 are sensible, and **PP** is recommended
