## Question

> What are the distinct classes of torts under Common Law? Create a hypothetical situation of tort claim under each one of the distinct types.

> [!warning] **Must give a hypothetical for each of the 3 types.** Naming them alone is not enough.

## Quick answer

Three types — I.N.S. memory aid:

| Type | Definition | Hypothetical |
|---|---|---|
| **Intentional** | Deliberate harm | A punches B → battery |
| **Negligence** | Careless but unintentional | Texting driver runs red light, injures pedestrian |
| **Strict liability** | Liable even without fault (esp. products) | Store sells defective blender that injures buyer |

## Key points to hit

1. Start with **definition of tort** ("wrongful act other than breach of contract...")
2. Tort vs Criminal distinction (parties, standard of proof, "liable" vs "guilty")
3. **3 types with examples** — the heart of the answer
4. Each type's defining feature (intent? carelessness? no-fault?)
5. Hypothetical for each one (your own scenario or use the ones above)
