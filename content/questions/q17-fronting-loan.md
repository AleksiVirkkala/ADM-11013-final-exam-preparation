## Question

> Describe a fronting loan (*préstamo subsidiado*).

> [!warning] **You should be able to draw the diagram cleanly from memory.** Teacher drew it twice in class.

## Quick answer

A loan between a parent and subsidiary channeled through an **international bank intermediary**. The bank "fronts" for the parent to disguise the related-party nature.

**Two purposes:**

1. **Circumvent host-country capital flow restrictions** (host governments are less willing to block payments to international banks)
2. **Tax advantages** — interest paid in high-tax country is deductible; interest received in tax haven is tax-free

## The diagram (memorize)

```
   Tax Haven Sub (Bermuda, 0% tax)
            │
            │  Deposits $1M @ 8%
            │  (tax-free interest)
            ▼
       London Bank
            │
            │  Lends $1M @ 9%
            │  (tax-deductible interest)
            ▼
   Operating Sub (UK, ~50% tax)
```
