## Question

> What are the advantages of the floating exchange rate regime versus the advantages of the fixed exchange rate regime?

## Quick answer

**Floating advantages (3):**
1. Monetary policy autonomy
2. Automatic trade balance adjustments
3. Crisis recovery via depreciation

**Fixed advantages (4):**
1. Monetary discipline
2. Speculation control
3. Reduced uncertainty for international business
4. Trade balance argument is overstated (anti-floating point)

Economists disagree. Discuss both sides honestly.
