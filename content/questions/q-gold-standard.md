## Question

> When did the gold standard cease to exist?

(Teacher's review question — likely MC or short essay.)

## Quick answer — three dates, all mentioned

| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| **1933** | Roosevelt suspended the US **domestic** gold standard (during the Great Depression) |
| **1971** | Nixon ended **USD-gold** convertibility for foreign central banks |
| **1973** | **Bretton Woods** system formally collapsed; floating rates began |

> If unsure which the question targets, mention all three. You can't lose points for thoroughness.

## Key points to hit

1. **1914** — most countries left gold at WWI (war finance)
2. **1919-1928** — returned (US, UK, France)
3. **1933** — US Roosevelt eliminated domestic gold standard (Great Depression stimulus)
4. **1944** — Bretton Woods replaced with USD-gold anchor system
5. **1971** — Nixon ended dollar-gold link under speculative pressure
6. **1973** — Bretton Woods formally collapsed; floating rates emerged
7. **1976** — Jamaica Agreement formalized the floating regime
