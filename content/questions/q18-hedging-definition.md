## Question

> Explain the concept of hedging.

## Quick answer

**Hedging is protection against foreign exchange risk** by buying futures contracts (forward contracts, currency swaps, options) in foreign currencies.

## Full essay answer

See [[../topics/fx-exposure/hedging#Q18 essay answer|Hedging page — Q18 essay]].

## Key points to hit

1. **Definition**: protection against FX risk via futures contracts
2. **Etymology** (impressive detail): a "hedge" was a row of bushes protecting property lines in medieval Europe
3. **Hedge fund ≠ hedging** — different concept (high-risk diversified portfolio)
4. **Techniques** for transaction/translation exposure: forwards, swaps, options, lead/lag
5. **Economic exposure requires strategic hedge** — disperse production globally (Toyota example)
