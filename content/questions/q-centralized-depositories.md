## Question

> How do multinational corporations use centralized depositories for global money management?

Teacher: *"Read or will go bad in the final exam. You'll lose a bunch of points."*

## Quick answer — 3 reasons to pool cash centrally

1. **Higher interest rates** on larger pooled deposits
2. **Better investment expertise** at a financial-hub depository
3. **Smaller required cash buffer** (variance math: uncorrelated subsidiary needs mean pooled σ is less than the sum of individual σ)

## Key points to hit

1. The question: pool cash centrally vs let each sub hold its own
2. **3 reasons to pool** (above)
3. **Variance math**: σ_pool = √(σ₁² + σ₂² + ... + σₙ²)
4. **Worked example**: 3 subs with σ = 1, 2, 3 → individual sum \$18M precaution, pooled √14 ≈ \$3.74M → saves \$6.8M
5. **Limits**: capital controls, transaction costs; many firms compromise (central for precaution, local for day-to-day)
