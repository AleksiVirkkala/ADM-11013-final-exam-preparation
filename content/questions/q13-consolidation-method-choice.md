## Question

> How does a firm determine which method it will use for subsidiary financial statement consolidation? Explain the logic of your answer.

## Quick answer

**The firm does NOT choose.** The method is determined by the subsidiary's **functional currency**:

| Functional currency = | Method |
|---|---|
| Home country currency (or inflationary country) | **[[../topics/consolidation/temporal-method\|Temporal]]** |
| Host country currency | **[[../topics/consolidation/current-rate-method\|Current Rate]]** |

Auditors enforce. The CFO can't pick whichever gives better-looking numbers.

## Full essay answer

See [[../topics/consolidation/functional-currency#Practice question Q13 (essay)|Functional currency page — Q13 essay]].

## Key points to hit

1. It's **not the firm's choice** — determined by economic reality (functional currency)
2. **Functional currency = home → Temporal**
3. **Functional currency = host → Current Rate**
4. Inflationary country → functional = USD → Temporal
5. Logic: the method should reflect the economic reality of the subsidiary
6. Auditors enforce, not management
