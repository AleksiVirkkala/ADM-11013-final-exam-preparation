## Question

> Explain the distinct classes of intellectual property.

## Quick answer

Three classes:

| Class | Right | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| **Patent** | New invention/process | ~20 years (WTO TRIPS) |
| **Copyright** | Authors/composers/artists | ~50 years after death (TRIPS) |
| **Trademark** | Brand names/logos | Indefinite while in use |
