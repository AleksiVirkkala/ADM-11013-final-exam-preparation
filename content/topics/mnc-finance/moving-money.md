> The 4 [[../tax-mechanisms/index|tax-reduction mechanisms]] viewed from a money-management lens (rather than tax-minimization lens). Same four techniques, different motivation.

## The four techniques

| Technique | Tax treatment | Main use |
|---|---|---|
| **[[../tax-mechanisms/dividends\|Dividend remittances]]** | Paid AFTER local income tax. Subject to dividend withholding tax. | Most common; standard cash repatriation |
| **[[../tax-mechanisms/royalties\|Royalty payments and fees]]** | Tax-deductible locally → reduces foreign sub's tax liability | Move money out via IP licensing |
| **[[../tax-mechanisms/transfer-pricing\|Transfer prices]]** | Manipulable to shift profits. Subject to arm's-length scrutiny. | Adjust internal trade prices to shift profit |
| **[[../tax-mechanisms/fronting-loans\|Fronting loans]]** | Tax-deductible interest. Circumvents capital controls. | Bank intermediary disguises related-party flow |

## Unbundling

**Unbundling** = using a *mix* of techniques rather than relying on one. Why:

- Looks less suspicious to host governments
- Minimizes tax exposure across multiple regulatory regimes
- Lets the firm respond to different jurisdictions' rules

## Tax burden management terms (likely MC)

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Tax credit** | Reduces home-country tax by the amount paid to foreign government |
| **Tax treaty** | Bilateral agreement specifying which country taxes which kind of income |
| **Deferral principle** | Parent isn't taxed on foreign-source income until a dividend is actually received |
| **[[../tax-mechanisms/tax-havens\|Tax haven]]** | Country with very low or zero corporate income tax (Bermuda, Bahamas, Cayman, Ireland 12.5%) |
| **Double taxation** | Income taxed by both host-country and home-country governments — mitigated by tax credits, tax treaties, and the deferral principle |

## Why "moving money" deserves its own framing

Beyond tax minimization, multinationals move money for:

- **Working capital management** — make sure each sub has enough cash to operate
- **Hedging currency exposure** — pull funds out of a depreciating currency
- **Investment opportunity** — redeploy cash from a low-return sub to a high-return sub
- **Capital control workarounds** — get money out before host country restricts it
