| Concept | Key takeaway |
|---|---|
| [[lessard-lorange\|Lessard-Lorange]] | Use **projected (forward) rate for BOTH budget AND actual** |
| [[centralized-depositories\|Centralized depository]] | Pool cash centrally for 3 reasons: higher rates, expertise, smaller pooled buffer |
