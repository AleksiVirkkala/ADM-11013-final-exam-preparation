## At-a-glance summary

| Exposure | Effect | Example | Hedge |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Transaction** | Individual deal P&L | US airline orders 10 Airbus A330s priced in EUR; €/$ moves before delivery | Forward, swap, lead/lag |
| **Translation** | Reported financials (accounting) | Peso depreciation lowers USD value of Mexican sub's equity in parent's books | Forward, swap, lead/lag |
| **Economic** | Long-run competitive position | Strong USD → US exporters become uncompetitive | **Strategic** — disperse production globally |
