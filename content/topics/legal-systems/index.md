## At-a-glance

| Concept | Quick reference |
|---|---|
| Common law | UK, US, Canada — judge-made via precedent; flexible; detailed contracts |
| Civil law | Germany, France, Mexico — code-based; predictable; shorter contracts |
| Theocratic | Islamic countries — religious foundations |
| Torts (3 types) | Intentional · Negligence · Strict liability |
| IP (3 classes) | Patent (~20 yr) · Copyright (~50 yr after death) · Trademark (indefinite) |
| FCPA | US 1970s law — bribing foreign officials illegal; "facilitating payments" exception |
