## Critical exam tips at a glance

> [!danger] **IFE formula book error** — the book on p. 300 has **S₂ in the denominator**. It's wrong. Use **S₁**:
> $$\frac{S_1 - S_2}{S_1} \times 100 = i_D - i_N$$
> See [[topics/fx-theories/international-fisher-effect|IFE]] for details.

> [!warning] **Must-include phrases the teacher listens for**
> - Currency swap → "**with 2 different value dates**"
> - Currency board → "**100% reserves**"
> - Consolidation FX result → **must label "gain" or "loss" explicitly** (just the number = no credit)
> - Functional currency → teacher gives it, **you derive the method**

> [!tip] **Strategy:** Don't be superficial on essays. The teacher said this repeatedly: "If you lost points on the 2nd exam, it was probably because you were superficial. Write thorough, professional, in-depth responses."
