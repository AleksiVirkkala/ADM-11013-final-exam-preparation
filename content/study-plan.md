Skip the deep dives. Focus on the high-leverage essentials:

## If you have ~3 hours (day-before crunch)

1. **1.5h** — Master the [[topics/consolidation/exam-template|Consolidation template]]. Do the [[topics/consolidation/worked-inflationary|Inflationary exercise]] from memory.
2. **45m** — [[formulas|Formula sheet]] + [[cheat-sheet|Cheat sheet]]
3. **45m** — Skim every cluster's index page. Write out the [[topics/tax-mechanisms/index|4 tax mechanisms]] and [[topics/legal-systems/torts|3 tort types]] from memory.

## The morning of the exam (30 min)

Read [[cheat-sheet|cheat sheet]] cover-to-cover. Re-derive the [[topics/fx-theories/international-fisher-effect|IFE formula]] on scrap paper to make sure it's locked in. Bring your calculator + 2 pens.

## What NOT to spend time on

The teacher confirmed these are **excluded** from the final exam:

- Balance of Payments
- T-MEC / USMCA / NAFTA
- Export plan / 8 stages of an export operation
- 5 export documents (packing list, bill of lading, invoice, certificate of origin, pedimento)
- Incoterms, letters of credit

These were on the partial exam — don't waste time re-studying them.
